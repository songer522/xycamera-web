### Highlights

- **Pro Manual Controls:** Hardware-validated lens switching (Ultra Wide, Wide, Telephoto, and virtual optical crops), manual ISO, shutter speed or shutter angle (`180°`), Kelvin & tint white balance, manual focus Loupe, live RGB/Parade/Luma histogram, and stereo audio metering.
- **Apple Log, ProRes & Custom LUTs:** Native Apple Log, Apple Log 2, HLG, and SDR recording in HEVC, H.264, and ProRes. Built-in 10-look video library, `.cube` 3D LUT import with automatic log-curve conversion, and real-time preview or burned-in ("Bake") grading.
- **Retro Video Looks & Photo Filters:** Seven authentic vintage video emulations—**Camcorder**, **8mm Film** (18 fps), **CCD** (30 fps), **Cinema Film** (24 fps), **Flip Phone** (15 fps), **Instant**, and **Tape**—complete with adjustable strength and classic date stamps, plus 10 bespoke Display P3 looks for Photo mode (48 MP, ProRAW/DNG, HEIF, JPEG).
- **Dual Camera Recording:** Simultaneous front and rear multi-camera capture in **Picture in Picture** (rectangle, square, or circle floating window with pinch-to-resize and drag placement) or **50/50 Side by Side**, saved as either **One video** (composited) or **Two videos** (separate full-frame tracks grouped in Media).
