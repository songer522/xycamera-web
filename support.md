## Quick Start & Feature Guide (English)

### 1. Capture Modes: VIDEO, PHOTO & DUAL
Switch modes using the bottom mode strip:
- **VIDEO:** Record in Apple Log, Apple Log 2, HLG, or SDR using HEVC, H.264, or Apple ProRes.
- **PHOTO:** Capture high-resolution stills up to 48 MP in HEIF, JPEG, or Apple ProRAW (DNG), with Flash, Self-Timer (3 s / 10 s), and 10 built-in Display P3 photo filters (`IRVELA 27`, `PELTRA 58`, `NOKTEL 29`, `ARANZE 72`, `ALBRA 06`, `VAUSEL 15`, `TAMBEL 33`, `ISVEN 11`, `OSKEN 07`, `LOSVEN 04`).
- **DUAL:** Record front and rear cameras simultaneously in **Picture in Picture** (with Rectangle, Square, or Circle window shapes, pinch-to-resize from 20% to 45%, and four-corner snap) or **Side by Side** (50/50 split). Open the Display panel to choose between **One video** (composited) or **Two videos** (each camera saved to its own full-frame file and paired in Media).

### 2. LUTs & Retro Looks
Tap the **LUT** button on the camera screen:
- **LUT Tab:** Choose from 10 original film-inspired looks (`KESTO 12`, `LISTRA 26`, `SOLWEN 52`, `DUSKEN 314`, `GRISA 09`, `ONYRA 48`, `AMBREL 30`, `NORDE 18`, `MIRELLE 21`, `TINTRA 03`) or tap **+** to import any 3D `.cube` LUT file (up to 65³, ≤ 10 MB). Toggle **Burn in LUT** (or **Bake into photo** in Photo mode) to bake the grade directly into your file, or leave it off to preview live while recording clean Log.
- **Retro Tab (Video up to 30 fps):** Emulate classic cameras with **Camcorder**, **8mm Film** (18 fps), **CCD** (30 fps), **Cinema Film** (24 fps), **Flip Phone** (15 fps), **Instant**, and **Tape**. Tap the readout pill to adjust **Strength** (0–100%), toggle **Burn in look**, or enable the classic **Date stamp**.

### 3. Pro Manual Controls
- **Exposure & Shutter Angle:** Tap **EV / ISO / Shutter** to switch between Auto and Manual exposure. In Settings, toggle **Shutter Angle** to read shutter values in degrees (such as `180°`) instead of fractions of a second.
- **White Balance & Focus:** Lock or scrub Kelvin temperature and Tint, or pull manual focus with smooth scrubber dials.
- **External USB-C Storage:** Plug in an external USB-C SSD formatted as exFAT or APFS to record directly to external storage (required by iPhone hardware for high-frame-rate ProRes formats such as 4K60 ProRes).

### 4. Quick Launch (Control Center, Lock Screen & Action Button)
You can launch XY Camera directly from the Lock Screen, Control Center, or the iPhone Action Button using the built-in **Quick Launch** control.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Why is "Burn in LUT" or "Burn in look" dimmed when ProRes is selected?**
Apple ProRes recording preserves the clean sensor Log/color stream directly via hardware encoding. While shooting ProRes, your chosen LUT or Retro look appears live in the viewfinder, and you can export a graded copy anytime from the in-app **Media** library.

**Q: Why are Retro looks unavailable at 60 fps or 120 fps?**
Retro looks emulate classic frame cadences (such as 18 fps for 8mm Film, 24 fps for Cinema Film, and 15 fps for Flip Phone) and operate at frame rates up to **30 fps**. Switch your frame rate to 24 fps, 25 fps, or 30 fps to enable the Retro tab.

**Q: How do I change the app language?**
Open **Settings › Language** inside the app to jump directly to iOS's per-app language selector, where you can choose from 13 supported languages (English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, and Indonesian).

**Q: What do the "Buy Me a Coffee" tips in Settings unlock?**
Nothing—every camera feature, LUT tool, Retro look, and Dual mode is included for everyone. Tips in Settings are completely optional one-time contributions to support ongoing independent development.
